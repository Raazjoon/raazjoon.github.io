The brief

Trust and clarity before the next step

PayRafi serves people navigating international money transfers, including freelancers, students, and Iranians abroad. The brief was to build a consistent social presence that explained the service and addressed the questions around using it.

Communication priorities

  • Explain how the service and transfer process work.
  • Address questions about safety, fees, and rates.
  • Connect education, product guidance, and campaign messages.

What I was responsible for

I organized topics, formats, channel emphasis, and publishing into a connected calendar, then designed and adapted the visual content within the existing identity.

How I approached it

Three decisions that shaped the work

01

Sequence the audience’s questions

The calendar connected problem awareness, safe guidance, trust, app education, and action. This gave educational and promotional pieces a place within a broader publishing plan.

  • How do I transfer safely?
  • Can I trust the service?
  • What is the offer?
02

Keep the framework shared. Adapt the execution

All three channels used the same content pillars. I varied the format, publishing frequency, and emphasis for each channel. Awareness was a stronger emphasis on LinkedIn and Telegram; trust and credibility were a stronger emphasis on Instagram.

ChannelAdaptation in practice
InstagramA visual hook, short carousel progression, and product-led guidance.
TelegramCompact summaries and direct messages around services and campaigns.
LinkedInStructured explanations and comparison-led content.
03

Make the strategy executable

I connected channel, format, topic, timing, and CTA in the content calendar. A shared structure supported cross-platform planning while leaving room for the different roles of each channel.

How I used measurement

I designed a framework across distribution, engagement, and intent, with platform-specific measures. Performance review informed questions about video length and how to support deeper audience engagement.

What remained to test

Shorter explainers and stronger bridges from discovery to consideration were next directions. The project ended before these hypotheses could be fully re-measured.
