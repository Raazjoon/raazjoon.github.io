The context

Stay useful between clinic visits

The clinic already had a visual identity and service-explanation content. Its ongoing Instagram activity needed to reinforce expertise, show that the clinic was active, and keep services relevant to existing and returning audiences.

The communication focus

  • Practical education informed by specialist experience.
  • Consistent Feed and Story activity.
  • Service reminders and reasons to reconnect.

A useful extension of the work

Some published educational posts became reusable assets in client advice: the specialist could send a relevant post directly to a client for further explanation.

How I approached it

Connect expertise, format, and follow-through

01

Use real questions as editorial inputs

I drew topics from seasonal context, specialist recommendations, and recurring client behaviors. Educational content moved through research, drafting, expert review, revision, design, and approval before publishing.

02

Build understanding across Stories

Stories were planned in sequences rather than as isolated items. This four-frame example opens with a familiar question, introduces the topic, groups the key information, and closes with a practical next step.

03

Connect publishing with community care

I handled routine inquiries using standardized responses for appointments, location, packages, and service information. Questions requiring nutrition or device expertise went to the relevant specialist.

The brand identity and Feed cover system already existed. My contribution was the content planning, development, visual execution, and ongoing management within that system.

Selected deliverables

A consistent presence across formats

Educational Feed content and more conversational Stories used the existing visual language in different ways.

Observations from the work

I recalled stronger saves and shares around educational content, and more direct questions from conversational Stories. These patterns were not validated by the recovered Insights.

A reasonable next test

Compare conversational Story formats and service reminders across a broader sample, while keeping topic, timing, and distribution context in view.

Scope and specialist collaboration
  • Owned: content planning, topic selection, Feed and Story content structure, research and drafting, visual execution, scheduling, publishing, and community management.
  • Specialist input and approval: nutrition recommendations, content accuracy, and questions requiring clinical expertise.
  • Existing inputs: overall brand identity and Feed cover system.
